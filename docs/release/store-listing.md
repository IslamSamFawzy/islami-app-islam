## Full description

### English

Islami keeps the day's prayers, the Quran and the adhkar in one quiet place —
and almost all of it works with no connection at all.

**Prayer times and the adhan**
See today's schedule and the countdown to the next prayer. Choose which prayers
call the adhan — all five, or only the ones you want — and it sounds at the
right minute, with the full recitation, even when the app is closed. A month of
times is downloaded at once, so after the first run the schedule is there
offline.

**The Quran**
All 114 suras, with search that understands both English and Arabic (type
"بقره" or "Baqarah" or just "2"). Recently read suras open where you stopped.

**Fifty hadiths**
Fifty hadiths in Arabic, each with the narration it comes from, one card at a
time in a carousel you can swipe through.

**Recitations and radio**
Listen to Quran radio stations, or pick a reciter and play any sura. Download
the ones you want and they play offline, from the Downloads screen.

**Azkar, tasbeh and qibla**
Morning and evening adhkar, a tasbeh counter, and a qibla compass that works
from your last known location when you are offline.

**How to pray**
An illustrated step-by-step guide to the prayer, with the recitation for each
step and the hadith it rests on.

No account, no adverts, no tracking. What the app keeps, it keeps on your phone.

السور الـ114 كاملة، مع بحث يفهم العربية والإنجليزية (اكتب «بقره» أو «Baqarah» أو

إذاعات قرآنية، أو اختر قارئًا واستمع لأي سورة. حمِّل ما تريد ليعمل بدون إنترنت.
